👈 Tap the icons in the left rail — Hub · Tasks · Plan · Chat · Find · Store — to see the other real screens this tablet can reach. The Hub dashboard is the resting/home state; everything else is one tap away, same as swiping tabs on a phone.

Design Notes
Why a rail, not just the dashboard

A kitchen tablet parked on one static screen forever is less useful than one that can also become the full Quests board, Calendar, FindFam map, Chat, or Store on demand — this is the same 6 tabs the phone app has (Ask Cube stays a floating action, not a tab, matching how it already works everywhere else), just reachable via a persistent side rail instead of a bottom bar so the dashboard keeps its full width.

Type & scale

Fraunces (serif) carries the clock, headlines, and big numbers — warmer and more "kitchen" than a pure data-dashboard sans. Inter handles dense schedule/chore text. Everything sized up from phone scale for a few-feet viewing distance.

Not yet decided

Keep-awake / always-on-display behavior, an idle timeout back to the Hub dashboard after inactivity, whether PIN-switching still applies per-screen in kiosk mode, and how the existing bottom tab bar and this rail coexist (replace it in kitchenHub mode, or keep both?). Flag your reactions to the layout first — behavior comes next.
