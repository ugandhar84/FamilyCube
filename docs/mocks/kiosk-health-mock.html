Health and Records

  • Left menu: Medications (Today’s doses, All medications, Refills), Immunizations (Vaccines), Records (All records, Record a visit, Bring in a document, Scan a prescription), and Tools (Ask health AI, Export PDF). Red badges show overdue doses and records needing attention.
  • Whose records? A family chip row at the top of every area, the same as the phone’s people page. Everyone, or one person.
  • Today’s doses shows a pastel card per person with each dose time, Mark taken, overdue outlined in red, and a taken streak. All medications has the phone’s filters (Active, Taken today, Pending, Overdue, All), search, History with 14 days of taken or missed dots, Edit and Delete with confirmation.
  • Add medication has every phone field: name, who it is for, category, dosage and unit, frequency, times, days, start and end date, refill date, pills left, doctor, pharmacy, source, instructions, missed-dose alert after N minutes, who to alert, and ring like a call.
  • Refills shows pills left bars and the refill date, with Mark refilled. Vaccines shows series progress, administered by, location, next due, with Pending, Done and Due soon filters and Add vaccine.
  • Records has search and tag filters, an Encrypted badge, urgency, an AI summary, Download and a detail drawer with key findings and follow-up actions you can turn into an event or a chore.
  • Record a visit records, then Submit for analysis returns a summary, discussion topics (Important, Monitor, Info) and next steps you can add to the calendar. Bring in a document and Scan a prescription end in a “Check the findings” review with “I verified the source values” before anything is saved.
  • Ask health AI answers questions about the selected person. Export PDF has the phone’s review step: members, record types, doses included, format and a preview.
  • Kid sees only their own medications and can mark them taken. A teen or senior sees only their own medications, vaccines and records. Switch roles above.
