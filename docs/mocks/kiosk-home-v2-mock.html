Home

  • Parent Home: greeting with chips (waiting on you, plans today, weather), the Family Pulse card (who is sharing their location, with a dial), and Needs you: chores to Approve or Redo (asks for a reason), kid requests and reward requests.
  • Quick actions open the same right-side drawers as the phone: Task and Event (“just describe it”), Groceries, and Ride.
  • Right column: Next up (today’s events with who and the time, the current one outlined), Tonight’s dinner with who is cooking, and the Intercom to talk to a room.
  • Kid Home (Leo): my pulse with the chores dial, the coin count and a streak, My day as a big tap-to-finish checklist with coins on each chore, my next events, tonight’s dinner, Ask a parent and Check in. A teen sees the same with their own events.
  • Finishing a chore sends it to a parent for approval, and the coins count up once approved. Switch roles above, and try Night dim: the idle and lock screens dim from 9 PM to 6 AM and brighten on touch.
  • Pastel cards, muted blue only for actions, and a bottom tab bar with the avatar switcher, matching the rest of the kiosk.
