Home · family board

  • Parent Home is the family’s day on one board. A column per person, hour by hour, with a “now” line that the board scrolls to. Events are pastel blocks by person; chores due at a time sit in as small checkable chips. Tap a block for its details.
  • Top strip: greeting and chips (waiting on you, plans today, weather), a row of recent memory photos, and four quick actions: ＋ Task and ＋ Event open the “just describe it” forms, ✨ Ask Fam opens the assistant, and 🚗 Ride.
  • Right side: Needs you (Approve or Redo with a reason, kid requests, reward requests), Tonight’s dinner and the Intercom.
  • Kid Home has two clear halves: My to-do (chores with the coins on each, tap when finished, it goes to a parent to approve) and Today’s schedule (their events in time order with the next one outlined). A small pulse on top shows chores done, the streak and the coin jar, then tonight’s dinner and Check in. Ask Fam and Ask a parent are in the top strip.
  • Teen gets the same to-do and schedule with their own items. Switch roles above.
