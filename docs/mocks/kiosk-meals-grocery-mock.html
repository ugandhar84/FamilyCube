Meals and Groceries · area menu layout

Everything from the phone, in areas

  • Left menu picks the area. Tonight’s dinner and the live shopping status stay visible at the bottom.
  • Week plan: week navigator, 7-day board with chef or “No chef”, Add meal, AI planner shortcut, “Add week to list”. Tap a meal for the recipe drawer: ingredients, steps, Add to grocery, Share recipe, Edit. The meal form has every phone field: title and emoji, type, date, time, chef, prep, servings, dietary tags, ingredients, steps, note.
  • Recipe book: search and dietary filters, pending recipes for a parent to approve or decline, Add to week, dictate and AI refine, browse library.
  • AI planner: preferences, options per day, pick up to 2 a day, Confirm plan.
  • List: AI restock and AI add suggestions with the estimated total, partner shopping status, kid requests to accept or decline, store sections with Move, select mode (move, delete, mark returning), receipt scan, pin store locations, recently bought with Undo, item detail.
  • Runs, History, Insights: create a run and check items off live, purchases by date, and spending charts.
  • A kid gets read-only meals, can add recipes for approval and request groceries, and has no AI planner or Insights.
