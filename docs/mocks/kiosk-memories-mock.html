Memories · area menu layout

Memories

  • Left menu: All memories, Most loved, My posts, On this month, and Ambient slideshow, with the count of unread new memories as a badge.
  • The wall is three pastel columns, newest first, with “Load more” at the end (the phone pages 12 at a time). Cards show the photo, the caption (or on the photo when the poster chose caption-on-photo), who posted, the date, the occasion, a heart count, a video badge and a count badge for multi-photo posts.
  • Filters: occasion chips (Milestone, Everyday, Celebration, Just because, and the family’s own occasions) and a person chip row. Search by caption.
  • Tap a memory for the detail drawer: a swipeable set of photos and videos, the caption, who is in it, “Loved by”, Love, Save to Photos, and Delete (the poster or a parent only).
  • New memory has Take a photo, Record a video (up to 3 minutes), Choose from gallery, up to 6 media with a star to pick the hero, a remove button on each, the story, a caption-on-photo toggle, the kind of moment (including your own), and who is in it. Posting notifies the family.
  • Ambient slideshow fills the screen and advances on its own, for a countertop glance.
  • Senior can love and watch but not post or delete. Kids and teens can post, and delete only their own. Switch roles above.
