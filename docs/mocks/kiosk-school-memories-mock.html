School and Memories

  • School has a left menu: Today, Week, Schedules, Holidays and exceptions, Scan a flyer. Today shows one pastel card per child with the day’s classes and dismissal, and “Open full day” slides in the full day drawer.
  • Schedules lists each child’s timetable with Edit periods and “Create school schedule” (school, grade, periods, days). Holidays has the list plus an add form (child, reason, from, to, all day).
  • Kid view sees only their own schedule, no create, scan or holiday editing. Switch to Leo above.
  • Memories is a three-column pastel photo wall with type filters (Milestone, Everyday, Celebration, Just because), a person filter, hearts with “Loved by”, and video badges.
  • Tap a memory for the detail drawer: big photo, caption, who’s in it, hearts, Save to library, Delete (parent or the poster). “New memory” offers camera, video, gallery, caption, type and people. “Ambient slideshow” fills the screen for a countertop display.
  • Senior (Nana) can heart and watch but not post or delete, matching the phone.
